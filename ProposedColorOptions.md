# Proposed Color Options

Here are three alternative color schemes that could elevate the look of your application.

## Option 1: "Modern Professional" (Refined & Trustworthy)
*A sophisticated evolution of your current scheme. Uses deep blues and teals for a sense of security and professionalism, perfect for an attendance system.*

### Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Royal Blue (`#2563eb` → `#1e40af`)
- **Accent**: Emerald Teal (`#10b981`)
- **Background**: Cool Gray (`#f8fafc`)
- **Surface**: White (`#ffffff`) with subtle borders (`#e2e8f0`)

### Why it works
- **Trust**: Blue is the standard for enterprise/admin tools.
- **Clarity**: High contrast between the deep primary and the light background.
- **Familiarity**: Users instantly recognize this as a "serious" tool.

## Option 2: "Vibrant & Bold" (Energetic & Engaging)
*A high-energy palette that uses gradients and brighter saturation. Great if you want the app to feel more like a modern consumer product or startup.*

### Palette
- **Primary**: Electric Violet (`#7c3aed` → `#6d28d9`)
- **Accent**: Hot Pink / Magenta (`#db2777`)
- **Background**: Very Light Purple Tint (`#faf5ff`)
- **Surface**: Pure White (`#ffffff`) with high drop shadows

### Why it works
- **Engagement**: Bright colors keep users awake and engaged.
- **Modernity**: Violet/Pink gradients are very trendy in SaaS right now.
- **Distinctive**: Stands out from typical "boring" admin panels.

| **Success** | Mint Green | `#34d399` |
| **Warning** | Orange | `#f97316` |
| **Error** | Red | `#ef4444` |

---

## Option 3: "Minimalist Dark" (Sleek & Focus-Driven)
*A dark-first aesthetic (or high-contrast light) that reduces eye strain and focuses entirely on the content. Think "Developer Tools" or "Dashboard" vibes.*

### Palette
- **Primary**: Monochrome / Off-Black (`#18181b`)
- **Accent**: Neon Blue (`#3b82f6`) for active states only
- **Background**: Pure White (`#ffffff`) or Deep Zinc (`#09090b`) in dark mode
- **Surface**: Light Gray (`#f4f4f5`)

### Why it works
- **Focus**: Removes color distraction; color is used *only* for status/action.
- **Cleanliness**: Looks extremely professional and data-heavy friendly.
- **Timeless**: Black and white never goes out of style.

### Color Codes
| Role | Color | Hex |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Zinc Black | `#18181b` |
| **Secondary** | Zinc Gray | `#71717a` |
| **Success** | Success Green | `#22c55e` |
| **Warning** | Warning Yellow | `#eab308` |
| **Error** | Error Red | `#ef4444` |

---

## Recommendation
For an **Attendance Management System (FRAMS)**:
- **Option 1 (Modern Professional)** is the safest and most appropriate choice. It builds trust and clarity.
- **Option 3 (Minimalist)** is great if you have complex data tables and want to reduce visual noise.
